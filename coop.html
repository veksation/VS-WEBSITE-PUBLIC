Co-op

Want to enjoy Vex & Shell with a friend? Say less! Vex & Shell will feature a Co-op mode where you and a friend can lean back and relax on your couch and stop the BBI together! Co-op will be featured in the demo as a working prototype for player feedback!

Vex & Shell is designed for Co-op. Control Vex and Shell independently in most scenarios, including during battles. Discover exclusive dialogue interactions depending on whether Vex or Shell starts a conversation (and that's not even limited to co-op!).
