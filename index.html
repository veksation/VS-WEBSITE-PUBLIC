What is Vex & Shell?

The story

Vex & Shell: a retro-inspired, turn-based RPG with a riveting story and original art style.

In the world of Vex & Shell, the BBI reigns terror. William Business, the perfectionist leader and founder of the BBI, has taken great lengths to create a perfect world.

Play as Vex, the pacifist, and Shell, the pessimist, as you set out to stop him.

Features

  • Turn-Based Combat

    A riveting combat system that combines the best of classic turn-based battles with bullet-hell combat and unique per-fight gimmicks.

  • Immersive Dialogue

    Animated conversations with dynamic blinking and talking portrait effects as well as human-voiced dialogue sound effects.

  • Imperfect Artstyle

    An asymmetrical, hand-drawn art style that aims for imperfection, because the world is an imperfect place.

  • Graphical Shifts

    Several unique art style shifts throughout the game, some you already know, others you'll just have wait and see.
